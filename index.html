Nutrition estimate

Estimated from this recipe's ingredients using USDA FoodData Central (generic foods). Indian spices, ghee, ginger-garlic paste and plain Greek yoghurt use a built-in table with correct gram weights, so they are never matched to the wrong food. Other amounts are converted to grams where the database knows the portion, or with a standard tablespoon/teaspoon weight where it doesn't. It's a close estimate, not lab values — branded products vary, which is why your typed-in nutrition always wins unless you save an estimate over it.
